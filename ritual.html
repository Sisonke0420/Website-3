Field note / 08.28.26 / 7 min

Build a running ritual that lasts.

The best training plan is the one that gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

Consistency is less about discipline than design. Give your run a shape that feels easy to return to, and the miles begin to gather on their own.

Start with a repeatable cue

Put your shoes where you will see them. Choose one familiar loop. Keep the first ten minutes deliberately easy. A small cue removes the daily negotiation and leaves more attention for the run itself.

Protect the feeling

Not every session needs to be a test. Finish with enough energy to feel curious about tomorrow. Over time, that reserve becomes the foundation for longer, faster work.

Build a ritual that belongs to your real life, then let the distance grow at its own pace.
