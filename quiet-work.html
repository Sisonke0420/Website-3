Recovery / 5 min

The quiet work after the finish.

Recovery is not the pause between efforts. It is part of the effort.

The finish line gives you a result. Recovery gives you the chance to use it. Treat the hours after training as a deliberate part of the process.

Bring the system down slowly

Walk for a few minutes, drink before thirst catches up, and choose a meal that feels practical rather than perfect. Small signals tell the body that the work is complete.

Pay attention to tomorrow

A good recovery plan is honest about the next session. Mobility, sleep, and an easier day are not signs of weakness; they are how a strong week stays intact.

Take care of the person who has to wake up and do it again.
