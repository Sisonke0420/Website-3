How to make easy miles count.

The quiet sessions are where your body learns to keep going.

Easy does not mean empty. A low-intensity run builds the aerobic engine, clears the mind, and gives hard sessions somewhere to land.

Keep the pace honest

Use your breath and conversation as your guide. If the pace makes you reach for the next minute, it is probably too fast for an easy day. Leave room for control.

Let the volume accumulate

One relaxed run may feel small, but a month of them changes your baseline. Keep the route familiar, carry what you need, and let the steady work become visible in the sessions that follow.

The goal is not to prove fitness every day. It is to make fitness durable.
